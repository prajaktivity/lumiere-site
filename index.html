Light is the main ingredient

Lumière the Shine is a photography studio that treats light as the secret ingredient. We start with food — styling, texture, steam and sparkle — to help restaurants, cafés, bakers and home chefs show their work at its most delicious.

Soon, the studio expands into portraits, model portfolios, newborn sessions and more. The goal stays the same: make every frame glow.
